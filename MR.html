📌 Overview

The Great Microwave Debacle refers to the chaotic events of October 14, when Alex attempted to heat up a single slice of foil-wrapped leftover pizza in the group chat's shared apartment space.

The resulting sparks created a minor power outage in the kitchen, leading to 3 weeks of continuous chat teasing and the creation of the :fire_microwave: custom emote.

💬 Chat Log & Quotes

"Wait, guys, are metal forks allowed in the microwave if they're touching the food?" — Alex (3 seconds before disaster)
"I didn't burn the house down, I just gave the pizza dynamic lightning effects." — Alex (defending themselves in voice chat)
